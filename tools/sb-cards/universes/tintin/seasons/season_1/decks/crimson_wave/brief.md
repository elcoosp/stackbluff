# Crimson Wave — The Ligne Claire Master Brief

## RESEARCH-BASED STYLE DIRECTIVE
**Based on the artistic definition of *Ligne Claire* pioneered by Georges Remi (Hergé).**

To achieve a true Hergé look, the AI must adhere to these strict rules:
1.  **Uniform Line Weight:** Every outline in the image must be identical in thickness. There are no thick "outer" lines and thin "inner" details. Every contour is drawn with the same consistent, medium-width black ink line.
2.  **Flat Coloring (No Shading):** Colors are solid blocks of paint. There are **no gradients, no airbrushing, and no hatching**. Shadows, if any, are represented by a completely different, darker solid color, not a fade.
3.  **Geometric Construction:** Characters are built from simple shapes (circles, ovals, cylinders). The style is clean, architectural, and precise.
4.  **Vintage Palette:** Use bright, slightly saturated primary colors (Signature "Hergé Blue," rich reds, warm yellows) often separated by thick black outlines.
5.  **2D Flatness:** The image should look like a printed comic book cell, not a 3D render. No depth of field, no lens flares.
