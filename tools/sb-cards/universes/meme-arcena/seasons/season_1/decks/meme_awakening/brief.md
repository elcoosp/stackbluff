# Meme Awakening — Design Brief

## DECK NAME
Meme Awakening

## COLOR PALETTE
- Primary dark (Rim color): #1A2B3C
- Primary accent (Frame color): #5D9B9B
- Secondary accent: #E0D68A

## VISUAL STYLE & LAYOUT RULES
MANDATORY POSITIVE KEYWORDS: internet meme style, bold black outlines, flat vector illustration, no shading, pure solid colors, exaggerated facial expressions, digital art, clean lines, white background.
MANDATORY NEGATIVE KEYWORDS: 3D render, realistic, shading, gradient, soft edges, watercolor, photo‑realistic, complex background, shadows, depth, anime, manga, Moebius, Jean Giraud.
Character busts (Kings, Queens, Jacks, Aces, Jokers): 9:16 vertical, solid white background, symmetrical frontal pose (except jokers in side profile).
No rank letters, no suit symbols, no card borders, no text on character illustrations.

## HIDDEN GEMS SYSTEM
1. **Narrative Arc:** The SPADES suit tells a story from 2 to 10.
   - 2SPADES: Average Wojak stares at a cracked phone screen, trapped in an endless scroll of normie content.
   - 3SPADES: Doomer Wojak appears, whispering that the grind is pointless and the void is inevitable.
   - 4SPADES: The protagonist tries to escape his feelings, morphing into a Soyjak – exaggerated tears and flailing.
   - 5SPADES: Wifejak gently places a hand on his shoulder, offering a warm cup of tea and a knowing smile.
   - 6SPADES: Smug Wojak challenges an NPC in a debate, winning with a single raised eyebrow and a folded paper fan.
   - 7SPADES: Clown Pepe (Honkler) appears, honking a horn and scattering chaotic energy, confusing both hero and enemy.
   - 8SPADES: The hero is tempted by the Stonks Meme, who presents a rising chart and whispers of easy wealth.
   - 9SPADES: Monkey Puppet slowly turns its head, giving the side‑eye that exposes the hero’s self‑deception.
   - 10SPADES: GigaChad emerges from the rift – jaw sharp, aura glowing – representing the final ascension from normie to legend.
2. **Recurring Mascot:** Glitch Sparrow – A tiny pixelated bird with one glowing blue eye and one red, leaving faint digital afterimages. Hidden on every face card and the typography grid.
3. **Reflection Secret:** The two Jokers overlay – Left: Sadge Pepe, crying alone in a dark room, face half‑visible / Right: WidePeepoHappy, ecstatic and glowing, facing the opposite direction

## CUSTOM PIP DESIGNS
The four suit symbols follow the clan visual DNA of the suit they belong to (meme style).
- **SPADES** : puzzle pieces, sharp edges shape, interior grid of tiny dots, edge eye motifs and question marks, color #1A2B3C
- **HEARTS** : soft, rounded, organic shape, interior subtle wavy lines, edge heart-shaped corner flourishes, color #E25E7C
- **DIAMONDS** : jagged, unpredictable, asymmetrical shape, interior splatter / drip, edge shattered mirror fragments, color #F4C542
- **CLUBS** : geometric, rigid, angular shape, interior crosshatch / steel grain, edge gear teeth and rivets, color #0F0F12
