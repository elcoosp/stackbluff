### 🧬 Deep Lore Integration

**1. The Looksmaxxing Tragedy:**
The core tragedy of the Disruptors is embodied in the **Looksmaxing Chudjak** and the **Ripped Chudjak**. They are trying to "ascend" to become **Gigachad** or even **Classic Chad**. But as the "Ripped Chudjak" shows, you can have the body of a god, but if your soul (or face) is a Chud, you will never be King. This creates a visceral, body-horror element to the Disruptor faction—they are mutilating themselves to fit an AI-generated ideal.

**2. The Larp War:**
Notice the parallel between **Viking/Crusader Chad** and **Viking/Crusader Apu**. The Apus are cosplaying as the Chads. This suggests that the "New Blood" has no identity of its own; it can only mimic the glory of the Old Guard, but in a smaller, softer, less convincing way. The war is essentially a fight between the "Originals" and the "Fan Art."

**3. The Economics of Soy:**
The **Crying Economics Soyjak** and **Soylana Crypto Soyjaks** explain *why* the Reactive Class is so volatile. They are financially invested in the outrage. If the war stops, the "Drama Coin" crashes. They *need* the Old Guard and Disruptors to fight so they can farm engagement to pay their rent (referenced by the "Rent Is Too Damn High Wojak").
To make this a true epic, the narrative cannot just move forward in time—it must move forward *across four distinct fronts*. By assigning a suit to each faction, the 2-10 number cards tell four parallel, escalating stories that converge in the face cards.
