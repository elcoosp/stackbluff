## HIDDEN GEMS SYSTEM
1. **Narrative Arc: Clubs.** The Clubs suit (2–10) tells the story of “The Nocturnal Bloom” — a Victorian poison garden’s toxic transformation under moonlight. The 2 is a single belladonna berry; 3 is a crushed foxglove leaf; 4 is a spilt vial of midnight oil; 5 is a rusted trowel; 6 is a single thorn; 7 is a wilting black rose; 8 is a spiderweb draped over aconite; 9 is a cracked apothecary jar; 10 is a fully bloomed, glowing gardenia with poisonous silver veins.

2. **Recurring Mascot: The “Ashen Moth.”** A tiny, silver-veined moth with tattered, lace-like wings, no larger than a pip. Hidden once on every face card (12 total): perched on a crown, resting on a velvet glove, nestled in a thorn vine, or clinging to the reflection of a poison bottle.

3. **Reflection Secret: The two Jokers form a “Cracked Specimen Jar.”** The left Joker (“Curator”) shows a gloved hand holding an intact glass jar containing a single preserved gardenia. The right Joker (“Release”) shows the same hand dropping the jar, the glass shattering outward. Overlaid, the jar’s outline and the flower align into one complete image.

## CUSTOM PIP DESIGNS
- **♠ Spade:** A spade shaped like a pointed shovel for a poison garden. Interior: fine, parallel cross-hatching suggesting rusted iron. Edge: a single, unbroken line with slight swelling at the top. Color: #1C1B1E. No glow.
- **♥ Heart:** A heart formed by two overlapping belladonna berries. Interior: stippled dot texture in deep bruised purple. Edge: a sinuous, double-line mimicking a vine. Color: #B82B4B. No glow.
- **♦ Diamond:** A faceted diamond shaped like a cut-glass apothecary vial. Interior: rough, granular texture with radiating engraved lines like liquid swirling. Edge: a crisp, double-outlined border. Color: #B82B4B. No glow.
- **♣ Club:** A club shaped like a gnarled, thorned branch from a black rose bush. Interior: tightly woven cross-hatching. Edge: a chunky, irregular line with visible ink pooling. Color: #1C1B1E. No glow.
