## HIDDEN GEMS SYSTEM

### 1. Narrative Arc: Clubs

The Clubs suit (2 through 10) tells *“The Nocturnal Bloom”* — a Victorian poison garden’s toxic transformation under moonlight.

| Card | Story element |
|------|----------------|
| 2♣ | A single belladonna berry |
| 3♣ | A crushed foxglove leaf |
| 4♣ | A spilt vial of midnight oil |
| 5♣ | A rusted trowel |
| 6♣ | A single thorn |
| 7♣ | A wilting black rose |
| 8♣ | A spiderweb draped over aconite |
| 9♣ | A cracked apothecary jar |
| 10♣ | A fully bloomed, glowing gardenia with poisonous silver veins |

### 2. Recurring Mascot: The Ashen Moth

A tiny, silver‑veined moth with tattered, lace‑like wings, no larger than a pip.

**Hidden locations:**
- Once on every face card (12 total)
- Once within the **2×13 typography grid** (hidden in the linework of one character in either the top or bottom row)

**Typical positions:** perched on a crown, resting on a velvet glove, nestled in a thorn vine, or clinging to a poison bottle reflection.

### 3. Reflection Secret: The Cracked Specimen Jar

The two Jokers form one complete image when overlaid.

| Joker | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Left Joker (“Curator”) | Gloved hand holding an intact glass jar with a preserved gardenia |
| Right Joker (“Release”) | Same hand dropping the jar, glass shattering outward, gardenia falling |

Overlaid, the jar’s outline and the flower align perfectly.

## CUSTOM PIP DESIGNS

### ♠ Spade
- **Shape:** Pointed poison‑garden shovel
- **Interior:** Fine parallel cross‑hatching (rusted iron)
- **Edge:** Single unbroken line with slight top swelling
- **Color:** #1C1B1E · No glow

### ♥ Heart
- **Shape:** Two overlapping belladonna berries
- **Interior:** Stippled dot texture in deep bruised purple
- **Edge:** Sinuous double‑line (vine)
- **Color:** #B82B4B · No glow

### ♦ Diamond
- **Shape:** Faceted cut‑glass apothecary vial
- **Interior:** Rough granular texture with radiating engraved swirls
- **Edge:** Crisp double‑outlined border
- **Color:** #B82B4B · No glow

### ♣ Club
- **Shape:** Gnarled, thorned black‑rose branch
- **Interior:** Tightly woven cross‑hatching
- **Edge:** Chunky irregular line with ink pooling
- **Color:** #1C1B1E · No glow

## UNIFIED TYPOGRAPHY GRID (Single 2×13 image – dark row + accent row)

We generate **one** 16:9 image with two rows of 13 characters each.
- **Top row:** characters in **primary dark** (#1C1B1E)
- **Bottom row:** same characters in **primary accent** (#B82B4B)
- **Order left to right:** A, K, Q, J, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10
- **Tool:** Ideogram 3.0 (best for reliable text layout)

### [grid-typography] — LANDSCAPE (16:9)

**Positive:** A 2x13 grid of characters. Top row: 'A','K','Q','J','2','3','4','5','6','7','8','9','10' in color #1C1B1E (dark charcoal). Bottom row: same exact sequence in color #B82B4B (deep poisonous red). Each character is isolated with generous pure white space (at least 30% of each character's width) on both sides. No grid lines, no borders, no visible cells – only empty white background between characters. The characters are evenly spaced by whitespace. Vintage copperplate engraving style, intertwining thorned vines and tiny belladonna berries forming each character. The Ashen Moth (tiny silver‑veined moth) is hidden once within the linework of one character in either row. pure white background, high contrast, sharp lines, 16:9 aspect ratio.

**Negative:** grid lines, borders, frames, cell outlines, text outside the grid, lowercase, 3d render, shadows, dark background, grey background, blurry, misaligned, duplicate characters, missing characters, touching characters, overlapping characters, line break inside a row, more than two rows.

## FACE CARDS (12 cards)

**All face cards follow these rules:**

- **Aspect ratio:** Square (1:1)
- **Composition:** Waist‑up bust portrait only, flat bottom edge
- **Style:** Vintage copperplate engraving, traditional French‑suited playing card aesthetic
- **Background:** Pure white
- **Special rules:**
  - King of Hearts = holds weapon behind head (“Suicide King”)
  - King of Diamonds, Jack of Spades, Jack of Hearts = drawn in profile (one eye)
  - All others = forward‑facing
- **Hidden gem:** Ashen Moth appears once on each card

**Shared negative for all face cards:**
full body, legs, action pose, dynamic pose, scenery, landscapes, borders, card edges, text, letters, numbers, multiple figures, blurry, dark background
