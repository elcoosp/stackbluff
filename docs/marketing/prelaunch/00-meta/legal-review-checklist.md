## Pre-Post Legal Checklist

Every piece of content must pass ALL of these checks before posting. If any item fails, the post must be revised.

### A. ANJ (Autorité Nationale des Jeux) Compliance

- [ ] **No implication of real-money gambling.** The post must not suggest that chips have real monetary value, can be cashed out, or are equivalent to currency.
- [ ] **No "gambling" language.** Avoid words like "bet", "wager", "stake" (in a monetary sense), "win real money", "cash out", "withdraw".
- [ ] **No targeting of minors.** Content must not appeal specifically to people under 18. No youthful/childish imagery or language.
- [ ] **Play-money model is clear.** If a post discusses chips or purchases, the context must make clear this is play-money only.
- [ ] **No secondary market references.** Never suggest chips or cosmetics can be traded, sold, or transferred for real value between users.

### B. CNIL / GDPR Compliance

- [ ] **No PII in posts.** No real names, email addresses, IP addresses, or payment details in any post, screenshot, or crew log.
- [ ] **Screenshots are anonymised.** Any product screenshot must use fake data, not real user data. Usernames must be clearly fictional (e.g., "Player_01", not a real handle).
- [ ] **No tracking without consent.** Links in posts must not set tracking cookies before the user has consented on the landing page.
- [ ] **Data residency claims are accurate.** If we claim "EU-only data", this must be true at the time of posting. Do not claim compliance before it is implemented.

### C. Platform Policy Compliance

- [ ] **Telegram ToS.** No spam, no unsolicited mass DMs, no misleading bot behavior. The bot must clearly identify as StackBluff.
- [ ] **X/Twitter ToS.** No automated following/unfollowing, no coordinated likes, no misleading metrics claims.
- [ ] **Reddit Self-Promotion Rule.** Reddit posts must be < 10% self-promotional. Each post must provide standalone value. Read subreddit rules before posting.
- [ ] **Apple/Google (future).** No pre-announcing App Store availability before submission is approved.

### D. Intellectual Property

- [ ] **No copyrighted imagery.** AI-generated images must not clearly replicate copyrighted characters, logos, or art styles.
- [ ] **No trademark infringement.** Do not use competitor names in a misleading or defamatory way. "Unlike [Competitor]" is acceptable comparison; "[Competitor] is a scam" is not.
- [ ] **Font licenses.** JetBrains Mono and Inter are open-source (SIL/OFL). Verify any additional fonts are properly licensed.

### E. Financial Claims

- [ ] **No revenue promises.** Never imply that users can earn money, that the platform will reach a specific valuation, or that Season Passes are "investments".
- [ ] **No false scarcity.** "First 1,000 get triple" must be technically enforced. Do not claim limited availability if it is not actually limited.
- [ ] **Price accuracy.** If mentioning Season Pass price (5.99€) or Club Pro (4.99€/month), verify these match the BRS exactly.

---

## Review Process

1. **Content creator** (human or AI) generates post.
2. **Human founder** reviews against this checklist.
3. If any box is unchecked → revise and re-review.
4. Only the human founder can approve a post for publication.
