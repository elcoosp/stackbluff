## 1. The Core Concept

**Tell the story of five AI builders constructing something mysterious, reveal it as a poker platform built on constraints that become flexes, invite people to earn early access through referrals, and let the waitlist itself prove the viral engine works.**

The product is poker. The marketing is the heist. The budget is zero. The story is everything.

## 2. Campaign Name: "The Bluff"

Internal codename for the pre-launch narrative arc.

## 3. Target Audience (Primary)

- French Telegram users (18-45)
- Casual and enthusiast poker players
- Tech-curious developers (Rust, AI agents)
- Crypto/Telegram Mini App early adopters

## 4. Key Differentiators to Communicate

1. **Telegram-native** — `/poker` in any group = table in 10 seconds
2. **Zero pay-to-win** — Cosmetic-only monetisation. Chips are free.
3. **The Oracle** — AI coach that analyses your hand in 2 seconds
4. **Sub-10ms latency** — Rust actor model on a €5/month VPS
5. **Built by 5 AI agents** — The story IS the marketing

## 5. The Six Phases

| Phase | Name | Days | Emotional Target | Key Action |
|-------|------|------|-----------------|------------|
| 0 | The Void | 1–5 | Intrigue | Establish black accounts, first signal |
| 1 | Echoes | 6–20 | Curiosity | Builder voices emerge, feedback form |
| 2 | The Marks | 21–35 | Discovery | Suit symbols, codenames earned, leaked docs |
| 3 | The Reveal | 36–45 | Belonging | Product named, landing page, waitlist live |
| 4 | The Oracle | 46–60 | Competence | Daily Oracle readings, community contributions |
| 5 | The Deal | 61–75 | Exclusivity | Private beta, incident logs, championship |
| 6 | All-In | 76+ | FOMO → Action | Launch day, signing-off logs, stats |

## 6. Success Metrics (Day 75 Targets)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Telegram channel members | 5,000 |
| X/Twitter followers | 3,000 |
| Waitlist signups | 3,000 |
| Waitlist referral rate | 35% refer ≥1 |
| "Very disappointed" score | ≥ 40% |

## 7. The Pre-Launch Validation Tests

1. **Viral Coefficient Pre-Test:** Waitlist referral rate > 30% = BUILD
2. **Oracle Validation:** Oracle Reading engagement > 5% of followers = BUILD IT
3. **PMF Pre-Test:** ≥ 40% "very disappointed" if product never launched = BUILD
