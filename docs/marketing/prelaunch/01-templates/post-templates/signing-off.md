# Signing-Off Post Template

**Use for:** The final, dramatic crew logs on Launch Day (Phase 6).
**Platforms:** Telegram, X/Twitter

## Format

```
[SOUND_TAG] · Day 76

[SYMBOL] The [Codename]

─────────────────────────

"[Poetic, final summary of what they built.
 References their specific domain.
 Mentions another crew member.]

[Final sign-off line.]"
─────────────────────────

stackbluff.com
```

## Rules
- This is the emotional payoff. It should feel like the end of a heist movie.
- Must reference their specific domain (Architect = engine/math, Canvas = UI/frames, etc.).
- Must mention or nod to another crew member.
- The Human goes last.
